**`Figure4_dose_response`** — `main_figures/Figure4_dose_response.pdf`

Figure 4. Dose-dependent transcriptional response to progressive Fezf2 loss at P1. (A) Genes classified by dose-response pattern. (B) Het-versus-KO log2 fold-change space, coloured by class; the dashed diagonal marks a purely linear response. (C) Expression trajectories of the 8 strongest genes per class across the WT-Het-KO dosage series, centred on the WT level (bold line, class mean). (D) Volcano plot of KO versus WT differential expression at P1 (red, FDR < 0.05 and |log2 FC| > 1). (E) The 18 most strongly up-regulated compensatory genes. (F) Spearman correlation with Fezf2 dosage. Because the correlation is computed over only three genotype means, it can take just five values; compensatory genes are negatively correlated, i.e. they rise as Fezf2 is lost. Note: P1 is the only stage at which all three genotypes were sampled, and the dose statistics rest on three pseudobulk means, so these classes are descriptive rather than inferential.
